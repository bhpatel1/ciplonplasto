About Ciplon Plasto

Manufacturing excellence in Palanpur, Gujarat.

At Ciplon Plasto, we specialize in high-volume production of top-grade PET bottle preforms. Equipped with modern manufacturing equipment and strict quality checks, we supply preforms designed for maximum blow-molding efficiency.

Our Complete Product Range

We manufacture top-grade PET preforms tailored for diverse industry applications.

Mineral Water Preforms

Designed for lightweight blowing with high clarity and excellent neck strength. Popular in 30/25 and 28mm finishes.
