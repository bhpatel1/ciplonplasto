About Us

Built for precision, performance, and consistent supply.

We help beverage, food, and FMCG businesses move from packaging requirements to dependable PET preform supply.

Ciplon Plasto is a precision-driven PET preform manufacturer based in Palanpur, Gujarat. We cater to beverage, water, edible oil, dairy, and consumer packaging sectors with reliable products designed to meet global quality benchmarks.

Our team works with modern production systems, disciplined quality checks, and a customer-focused approach to deliver preforms that ensure clarity, strength, and uniformity in every batch.
